# Go-To-Market and Customer Acquisition Playbook

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

- **Primary Buyer**: Founder, Head of Operations, or After-Sales / CX Lead at a D2C consumer electronics, TWS audio, wearable, GaN charging accessory, or small smart-appliance brand.
- **Volume Band**: **500 to 50,000 hardware units shipped per month**.
- **Trigger Moments**:
  1. Monthly warranty and DOA claims cross ~50/month and overwhelm shared Gmail/WhatsApp threads.
  2. Multi-channel sales (Shopify/WooCommerce + Amazon/Flipkart + offline retail dealers) break storefront-only return widgets.
  3. A rogue supplier battery or PCB batch erodes margin before operations can isolate which factory lot failed.
  4. Chartered accountants flag missing separation between zero-GST OEM warranty replacements and taxable SAC 9987 Authorised Service Centre invoices.

---

## 2. Acquisition Loops Built Into the Product

1. **Discreet Branded Portal Referral Loop (`/w/[brand]`)**
   - Starter and Growth portals and digital warranty certificates carry a subtle `Protected with CircuitDesk` footer link seen by hardware founders, retail distributors, and partner service centres. Scale and Enterprise workspaces can toggle this badge off.
2. **Vertical Landing Surfaces (`/solutions/[vertical]`)**
   - Dedicated pages for `audio-wearables`, `mobile-accessories`, `smart-appliances`, and `d2c-electronics` speaking directly to coin-cell sag, BIS GaN batches, PIN bench routing, and dealer invoice verification.
3. **Search-Intent Operations Guides (`/guides/[slug]`)**
   - Technical playbooks on CBIC Circular 195/07/2023-GST warranty accounting, serial/lot failure tracking, Shopify warranty registration vs adjudication, and Indian 6-digit PIN service-centre routing.
4. **Zero-Friction Interactive Sandbox (`/demo`)**
   - Visitors launch a real database session across 5 RBAC roles, test QR registration, and run the live PIN router before booking a migration walkthrough.

---

## 3. Credible Path to $2,000 MRR (Target Unit Economics, Not a Forecast)

- **16 Growth brands x $129/mo (or INR 9,999/mo)** = **$2,064 MRR**
- **7 Scale brands x $299/mo (or INR 24,999/mo)** = **$2,093 MRR**
- **Onboarding Motion**: 6-step in-app activation checklist (`Brand -> SKU & Policy -> Serial CSV & QR Sheet -> Portal -> Teammate -> Test Claim`) paired with assisted CSV spreadsheet migration via `/contact`.
